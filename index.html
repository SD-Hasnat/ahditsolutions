We started by building our own products

AHD IT Solutions is based in Joypurhat, in northern Bangladesh. We did not start by pitching services — we started by building our own products, and the agency grew out of that work. Most of what we offer today is something we first had to design, code and ship for ourselves.

Our school management system runs every day at A H D Academy — admissions, attendance, fees, exams and report cards all go through it. Alongside it, we built an election candidate database covering Comilla and Joypurhat districts (673 candidate records) and a database of 325 private schools, kindergartens and coaching centres across Bogura.

We build on Google Sheets and Apps Script for many clients because it costs them nothing in server fees and runs on Google's own infrastructure. When a project needs more than that, we build it as a full web application instead.
